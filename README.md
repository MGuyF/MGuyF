## 🧑‍💻 About me — the way I'd tell it over a coffee

I'm **Guy Fleury Manirakiza**, known everywhere as **Manguy** — a full-stack developer with a **Burundian heart** and a keyboard that never really cools down. I fell for this craft because of one simple feeling: that moment when something you built makes somebody's day a little easier.

Since then I've been living happily between two worlds. The **front end**, where I obsess over spacing, motion and how a page *feels* in someone's hands. And the **back end**, where I design the data models, secure APIs and business logic that have to hold up when real users show up.

- 🔭 **Right now:** shipping full-stack apps with **React / Next.js / TypeScript** up front and **Django** or **Laravel** behind.
- 💼 **Track record:** end-to-end delivery of a production platform (Vue.js · Inertia.js · Laravel) — plus a shelf of personal products, all live and linked below.
- 🛠️ **My favourite kind of problem:** REST & GraphQL APIs, geospatial data (GeoDjango · PostGIS · MapLibre GL JS) and UI systems that stay clean as they grow.
- 🌱 **Always learning:** deeper TypeScript, testing, CI/CD and DevOps.
- 🤝 **Open to:** freelance missions, full-time roles and open-source collaboration.
- 📄 **The full story, live demos and my CV:** **<https://manguy-portfolio.vercel.app/>**

## 🇧🇮 Proudly Burundian — country code +257

Some things you don't choose, you just carry them with pride. I'm **Burundian**, and **+257** is the code that follows me everywhere I go: in my LinkedIn handle (`...bdi`), in the systems I build for my country, and now on this page. 🇧🇮

Burundi is a small country on the shores of Lake Tanganyika, but it's where my whole story with software began. I grew up there, earned my **Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering** there, and learned early that meaningful things get built with curiosity, discipline and iteration — not with the fanciest tools or the biggest budget. That's still exactly how I work today.

That's also why one of the integrations I'm proudest of is a compliance connector for **Burundi's OBR e-invoicing (eBM) platform**: making software that helps my own country's businesses run smoother is a different kind of satisfying.
